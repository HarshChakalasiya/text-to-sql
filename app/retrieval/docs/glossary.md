# SCF Business Glossary

- Buyer: corporate that purchases goods/services and may sponsor an SCF program
- Seller / Supplier: vendor that issues invoices to the buyer
- Tenor: number of days financing remains outstanding before maturity
- Basis points (bps): 1/100 of a percent; 100 bps = 1%
- Disbursement: cash paid to the seller under a financing
- Maturity date: date when the financing obligation is due from the buyer/obligor
- Credit rating: buyer's credit quality used for risk monitoring (AAA to BB in demo data)
- Hybrid search: combining keyword (BM25) and semantic (vector) retrieval for business documents
- Text-to-SQL: converting a natural language analytics question into a safe SQL SELECT
