# Payables and Receivables

From the buyer's perspective, approved invoices are accounts payable — obligations to pay suppliers. From the seller's perspective, the same invoices are accounts receivable — amounts expected from buyers.

Supply Chain Finance programs let approved payables be settled early for the seller (improving seller working capital) while the buyer keeps its original payment terms. The platform tracks this through invoices, financings, and payments tables.

Key questions:
- Outstanding payables by buyer: sum of invoice amounts that are not paid
- Receivables aging: invoices grouped by days past due for sellers
- Settlement status: whether payments fully cover invoice amounts
